What the flags mean

Each ticket can carry any of these. Tick more than one to see only tickets that have all of them.

Emergency

The ticket was tagged or labelled as an emergency.

Escalated

The MSE sent the ticket to a Medical Officer.

Probed

The MSE asked the caregiver for missing context before answering, or tagged a message Probed.

Closing lines like “any other questions?”, advice like “tell us if the fever returns”, and long answers with bullet points.

This rule is ours, not a Noora definition, so it can miss a probe or count a question that was not one. Hover the ? in a chat to see the line that triggered it.

Unmarked

None of the three flags above.
